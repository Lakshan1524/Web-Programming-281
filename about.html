FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data will remove it, so use Export CSV on the Tracker page to keep a backup.

How much should I save each month?

A common target is 20% of your income. If that feels too high, start with 5–10% and increase it as your habits improve.

What is the 50/30/20 rule?

Put 50% of income toward needs, 30% toward wants and 20% toward savings or debt repayment. Try it in the Budget Planner.

How big should my emergency fund be?

Aim for 3–6 months of essential expenses. Begin with a small starter fund and build it steadily using a savings goal.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Open common.js and change the cur value at the top of the FX object, for example to '$' or '€'.

Is FitXpence financial advice?

No. It is a tracking and learning tool. For investment or tax decisions, consult a qualified financial advisor.
